Transfer progress

  1. Transfer initiated

    Your transfer has been received. We're waiting for your payment to clear before processing.

  2. Processing

    Payment confirmed. We're converting your funds at the locked exchange rate and sending them to the recipient's bank.

  3. Funds sent to bank

    The funds have left SwiftSend and are in transit to the recipient's bank. The bank is processing the final credit.

  4. Delivered

    Your money has been successfully credited to the recipient's bank account. Transfer complete!

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my reference number?
Your transfer reference number is sent in the confirmation email immediately after you complete a transfer. It starts with SS- followed by the date and a unique code. You can also find it in your dashboard under Transfer history.
How long does an international transfer take?
95% of our transfers arrive the same business day. The exact time depends on the destination country and the recipient's bank. SEPA transfers typically arrive within 2–4 hours. USD and GBP transfers to most countries settle within 24 hours. Delivery estimates are always shown before you confirm a transfer.
My transfer is still showing as "Processing" — is something wrong?
Not necessarily. Processing times can be longer during weekends, public holidays, or if the recipient's bank requires additional verification. If your transfer has been in the "Processing" state for more than 2 business days, please contact our support team with your reference number.
Can I cancel a transfer?
You may be able to cancel a transfer if it is still in "Pending" status and we have not yet received your payment. Once a transfer moves to "Processing", cancellation is generally not possible. Please contact support immediately if you need to cancel a transfer.
