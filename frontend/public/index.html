How SwiftSend works

Four simple steps to send money anywhere in the world in minutes.

1

Calculate your transfer

Enter the amount you want to send. See the exact fee and exchange rate before you do anything.

2

Add your recipient

Enter your recipient's bank details. We support IBAN transfers and local bank routing for 30+ countries.

3

Pay securely

Fund your transfer by bank transfer or debit card. Your payment is protected end-to-end with 256-bit TLS encryption.

4

Money delivered

Your recipient gets the money in their local currency – usually within the same business day. Track progress in real time.

Why people choose SwiftSend

We built the service we wished existed when sending money home.

Real mid-market rate

We use the interbank exchange rate sourced from the European Central Bank – the same rate you see on Google. Banks typically mark up their rates by 3–5%. We don't.

One transparent fee

A small variable fee (from 0.7%) plus a fixed fee depending on currency corridor. That's it. No receiving-bank fees deducted from your amount, no hidden charges.

Safe and regulated

SwiftSend is authorised by the FCA as an Electronic Money Institution. Your funds are held in safeguarded accounts separate from our operating capital.
