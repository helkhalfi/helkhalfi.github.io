Simple, transparent pricing

We charge a small fee so we can give you the real mid-market exchange rate. That's it. No markup on the rate, no hidden costs — just one honest fee shown before you send.

Pricing FAQs

Are there any hidden fees?
No. We show you the complete fee — variable + fixed — before you confirm your transfer. We don't add any margin to the exchange rate. The rate you see is the rate you get, sourced directly from the European Central Bank.
Is there a minimum or maximum transfer amount?
The minimum transfer is £1 (or currency equivalent). The maximum per transfer is £1,000,000. For transfers above £50,000 we may require additional documentation as part of our compliance process.
Why is there a fixed fee in addition to the variable fee?
The fixed fee covers our banking and network costs for each transaction, which are roughly the same regardless of the amount you send. The variable fee covers our operating costs. Together they're still far lower than the rate markup a bank would charge.
Does the fee change if I use a card instead of a bank transfer?
Yes. Funding your transfer by debit or credit card adds a 0.4% card processing fee on top of the standard fee. Bank transfer funding is always free. We clearly show the total cost for each payment method before you confirm.
Will my recipient's bank charge extra fees?
SwiftSend does not charge your recipient's bank any fees. However, in rare cases certain recipient banks may apply their own incoming wire fees. We have no control over this and recommend your recipient checks with their bank beforehand if they are concerned.
Do I get a better rate for larger amounts?
All customers get the same mid-market exchange rate regardless of amount — there's no tiered pricing. Since the variable fee is a percentage, the fixed fee becomes proportionally smaller on larger transfers, making the effective rate even lower at scale.
